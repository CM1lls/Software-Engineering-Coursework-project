Ideas for features of Qin Shi Huang experience: 
Start up with a brief introduction of Qin Shi Huang (perhaps in the form of one of his advisors, would then take to the main chat experience)
Chat with Qin Shi Huang could be 'translated' as Qin Shi Huang himself would not be speaking in English, improves accuracy
Could introduce a trust system based on trust points, trust level determines tone of message
Perhaps at a high level, he would give information that he wouldn't at a lower trust level
A very low trust score could result in banishment/execution
Trust could be gained by appeasing to his political ideology 
Anything controversial would lower trust 
praise would gain trust
Main setting could be a throne room
Characters could be drawn in a very minimal style 
Could have a window onto a background where events take place (similar to Sort the Court)
Could have event messages like population/ gold in treasury change in response to decision 
Random events could occur in the background



Target demographic: History student 
Context of use: Museum Exhibit style experience, should be informative 
Constraints: Must be done by 15th December.

Process Models: 
Waterfall- could be a good idea to come up with a map of the process (i.e requirements -> design -> implementation -> testing) 
Agile - unlikely to be particularly useful, but could definitely get feedback and adapt the program
Scrum - Group roles could be a good idea for distributing workload (i.e. could have an organiser who does parts of everything, front end developed, back end developer, someone who focuses on the UI and visual elements of the program) 
Kanban - To do list could definitely be a good idea, could easily be adapted from the requirements list
